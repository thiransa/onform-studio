Onform works from brand identity to digital presence. We give businesses a shape people remember — a clear identity, a calm website, a product that feels as good as it looks.

The studio stays deliberately small. Every project is designed and built by the people you talk to, with no hand-offs and no account managers in between. That keeps the work close to the idea, and decisions fast.

With a foot in New York and one in Colombo, we work across two time zones — so while one city sleeps, the project keeps moving. Clients get the pace of a bigger team with the care of a small one.

We work with founders, independent labels, hospitality and product teams — from first sketch to launch. Our work so far spans fashion, fintech, interiors, pottery, photography and hospitality.

Every project starts from the same belief: good design is quiet. It doesn't shout for attention — it earns it, by being clear, considered and built to last longer than a trend.

How we work

(04)
  1. 01

    Discover

    We start with conversations — your business, your audience, and what makes you different. Research and references come before any design.

  2. 02

    Define

    We shape a direction: positioning, tone, and a visual territory to build from, agreed together before we go further.

  3. 03

    Design

    Identity, layouts and interfaces take form in focused rounds, with clear feedback points and nothing hidden.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We build, test and ship — then hand over files, guidelines and a site you can run yourself.
